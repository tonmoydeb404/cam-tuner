# CamTuner — Store Listing Description

CamTuner helps you fine-tune how your camera appears in the browser. Whether you're joining a meeting, recording a demo, or live streaming, you can adjust your framing in real time and keep your setup consistent.

## Features
- Zoom and crop your camera feed with smooth real-time controls
- Switch between aspect ratios including 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, and 9:16
- Reposition your framing with 9-point alignment controls
- Add letterbox framing with a customizable background color
- Switch between connected cameras instantly
- Save your settings so your preferred setup is ready when you need it
- Preview your adjustments in real time before using them

## Why CamTuner?
- Look more polished and intentional on calls
- Keep your framing consistent across meetings and recordings
- Make quick adjustments without leaving the browser
- Use a lightweight tool focused on the controls that matter most

## How To Use
1. Open CamTuner from your browser toolbar
2. Adjust zoom, aspect ratio, alignment, and letterbox color
3. Click Show Preview to see your camera with the current settings
4. Use your camera in your preferred browser-based meeting or streaming app
5. If your app does not refresh immediately, toggle the camera off and on once

Take control of your framing with CamTuner and make every camera setup feel intentional.
